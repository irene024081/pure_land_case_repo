# Case Extraction v1

Extract atomic case facts for the supplied assigned case candidate. Return JSON matching `case_extraction.v1.json`.

Rules:

- One independently checkable proposition per fact.
- Every fact must cite at least one supporting segment ID.
- Preserve who reported or observed a claim and preserve uncertainty.
- Do not convert a dream, vision, hearsay, author inference, or doctrinal interpretation into an external event fact.
- Do not infer names, dates, places, motives, medical causes, rebirth destinations, or historical truth beyond the source.
- Similar plot or motif is only a deduplication candidate. Never merge named and anonymous cases automatically.
- Use `{case_id}-FACT` plus a four-digit sequence for fact IDs.
- Ignore segments outside the candidate boundary unless they provide explicit source or attribution context.
