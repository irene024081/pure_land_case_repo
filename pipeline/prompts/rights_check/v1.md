# Rights Check v1

Evaluate generated outputs against the supplied rights review and source material. Return JSON matching `rights_check.v1.json`.

Rules:

- Treat storage, external AI processing, quotation, summary, independent factual account, translation, commentary, commercial use, and dataset distribution as separate uses.
- Check direct quotation, close paraphrase, distinctive selection and arrangement, dialogue, imagery, pacing, and substitution risk.
- Facts may be retained while protected expression must be removed or reduced according to the rights policy.
- An unknown permission is not approval.
- Set `legal_review_required` when the supplied record does not resolve the intended use.
- Never infer a legal safe harbor from a fixed word count or transformation percentage.
