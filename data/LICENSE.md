# Data Licensing

This repository contains mixed-rights data. There is no single license for the entire `data/` directory.

## CBETA-Derived Data

Normalized source text and adaptations derived from the CBETA XML P5 electronic database are distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), subject to CBETA's [copyright declaration](https://cbeta.org/copyright).

This applies to records whose provenance identifies CBETA XML P5 or `cbeta-org/xml-p5`, including the SRC0024 / T51n2072 material and the CBETA-delivered SRC0001 / X78n1549 material currently tracked in this repository. The four SRC0001 pilot entries delivered by Dazhouxian remain governed by their separate manifests and RR0005. The CBETA terms also apply to CBETA-derived portions of reports, run artifacts, translations, and other adaptations.

Required conditions include:

- attribute CBETA Foundation and identify the source text and version;
- link to the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license;
- identify material changes or transformations;
- use the material only for noncommercial purposes; and
- distribute adaptations under the same or a compatible license.

The underlying historical works may be in the public domain. This repository does not treat that fact as removing conditions associated with CBETA's modern electronic database. A commercial use must obtain additional permission or replace the CBETA-derived text with an independently sourced public-domain edition.

See [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) for the current attribution, version, and transformation record.

## Other Public Historical Text

Records based on other historical-text deliveries follow their recorded source-level and item-level rights reviews. A `public_domain_verified` field applies only to the identified historical text and does not cover modern annotations, site code, images, typography, or editorial assets.

## Restricted Modern Sources

Modern copyrighted source text under `data/source_entries/restricted/` is excluded from Git. Its tracked manifests and metadata do not grant permission to redistribute the underlying source text.

## Project-Created Metadata

Project-created metadata that is not covered by a source-specific license follows the repository's default terms in [`../LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md).
