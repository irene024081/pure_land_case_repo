# Copyright Research Workflow

Purpose: document the rights basis for collecting, processing, quoting, summarizing, translating, adapting, and publishing project material.

This is an operational research workflow, not legal advice. Copyright exceptions, duration, quotation rules, database rights, moral rights, and platform terms differ by jurisdiction. Escalate uncertain or high-impact publication decisions to qualified legal review.

## Working Definitions

### Copyrighted Work

An original expression fixed in a tangible medium, including articles, books, photographs, audio, video, transcripts, translations, editorial selections, and website text. Protection generally arises without a notice.

### Fact And Expression

Facts, dates, names, events, and general ideas may be extracted as information. The author's wording, selection, arrangement, dialogue, imagery, pacing, and other original expression may remain protected.

An `independent_factual_account` reconstructs supported facts in independently organized language. It must not reproduce distinctive expression or become a close substitute for the source.

### Adaptation And Derivative Use

Translation, dramatization, close rewriting, audiovisual adaptation, and some substantial transformations can implicate adaptation or derivative-work rights. Changing format or replacing words does not by itself create permission.

### Quotation And Copyright Exception

Quotation, research, criticism, teaching, and reporting exceptions vary by jurisdiction and depend on context. The project does not use a fixed word count or percentage as a legal safe harbor.

### Public Domain

A work may be free of copyright because protection expired or never applied. Verify the relevant work, edition, translation, annotations, images, and jurisdiction separately. A public-domain classical text does not automatically make a modern website edition or translation public domain.

### License And Permission

A license or direct permission may authorize specified uses. Record the exact grant, version, attribution requirement, modification rule, commercial limitation, territory, and revocation or update conditions.

### Platform Terms

Website and platform terms can restrict automated access, download, scraping, storage, or reuse independently of copyright ownership. A copy, share, print, or offline button is not automatically a republication license.

### Related Rights And Separate Risks

Copyright review does not replace review of:

```text
privacy and sensitive personal information
personality and publicity rights
medical confidentiality
defamation
contract and submission terms
moral rights and attribution
database rights
traditional or institutional restrictions
```

## Review Levels

### 1. Source-level Review

Research the default policy for a book, periodical, website, channel, archive, teacher, or institution.

```text
rights holder and publisher
license or permission statement
terms of service
jurisdiction
automated access policy
internal retention policy
AI processing policy
quotation policy
translation and adaptation policy
commercial use policy
contact route
review evidence and date
```

### 2. Source-entry Review

Determine whether an item inherits the source default or needs an override.

Override triggers:

```text
different contributor or copyright notice
submitted testimony with separate terms
repost or unclear source chain
third-party photographs, music, video, quotations, or translations
different license
unpublished or private material
special download or access condition
```

### 3. Input-object Review

Review each retained object separately:

```text
downloaded HTML or PDF
audio or video file
caption or transcript
OCR output
normalized full text
translation
image or screenshot
```

Permission to access one representation does not automatically authorize every other representation or use.

### 4. Generated-output Review

Review the proposed public output:

```text
metadata record
minimal quotation
reader summary
independent factual account
dharma case commentary
translation
creator brief
video script
downloadable dataset
```

The decision depends on both source policy and output behavior.

## Research Procedure

```text
identify the exact work and version
identify author, contributor, publisher, platform, and possible rights holder
locate copyright notice, license, terms, submission terms, and reuse statement
trace reposts and derivative sources to the earliest available source
separate site-owned content from third-party content
record relevant jurisdictions and intended publication territory
classify every intended use
save evidence URLs, locators, check dates, and archived evidence where permitted
apply the most specific item-level rule
record uncertainty and required permission contact
set a review expiry or event that triggers re-review
```

Absence of a copyright notice or reuse prohibition is not evidence of permission.

## Intended-use Matrix

Each rights review makes separate decisions for:

```text
access
automated retrieval
internal retention
AI extraction and analysis
internal full-text search
public metadata
public quotation
independent factual account
dharma case commentary
translation
commercial publication
dataset distribution
audio or video download
```

Allowed use in one column does not imply allowed use in another.

## Generated-text Risk Checks

For summaries, factual accounts, and commentaries, check:

```text
quantity and importance of copied wording
distinctive phrase similarity
paragraph and event-order similarity
reuse of dialogue, imagery, and narrative devices
whether the output substitutes for reading the source
whether restricted quotations appear in citations or debug views
whether doctrinal quotations comply with their own source policy
whether translation or close adaptation requires permission
```

Factual fidelity and rights safety are separate review dimensions. Rewriting more freely does not permit factual invention. Writing faithfully does not permit copying protected expression.

## Decision Status

```text
public_domain_verified
open_license_verified
permission_obtained
allowed_by_documented_exception
restricted_internal
metadata_only
legal_review_required
permission_required
prohibited
unknown
```

`allowed_by_documented_exception` requires a written jurisdiction-specific rationale and reviewer. AI cannot assign it without an approved rule or human legal review.

## Publication Gate

Before publication, verify:

```text
rights review is current
source entry inherits or overrides the correct source policy
every quoted source has an allowed quote policy
generated output type is permitted
expression-similarity and substitution checks pass
translation and adaptation policy is satisfied
attribution and license conditions are rendered on the page
restricted source content is absent from public payloads, logs, and debug views
```

Any unresolved high-risk condition results in `withheld`, `metadata_only`, or `legal_review_required`.

## Correction And Takedown

```text
retain the complaint or new evidence
withhold affected public content promptly
preserve internal audit records subject to lawful retention policy
identify all derived texts and translations
update rights status
regenerate or remove affected outputs
record the decision and restoration conditions
```
